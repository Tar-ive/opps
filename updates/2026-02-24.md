## Root Causes

### Why Quality Gate Failed

1. **Snippet Truncation:** Exa API search results contained partial text. Full pages had target language, but extraction snippets were incomplete.

2. **Deadline Interpretation:** Many top hackathons (HackIllinois, HackPSU, e-Fest) have March deadlines, which are < 7 days from Feb 24, 2026 baseline. **Strict filters exclude them.**

3. **Implicit vs. Explicit Language:** Candidates used phrases like:
   - "open to all students" (instead of "undergraduate")
   - "sponsor tracks with travel support" (instead of "travel reimbursement")
   - This triggers hard-filter rejections despite strong fit.

4. **Rolling/Evergreen Programs:** Several solid opportunities (Thiel, Emergent Ventures, Z Fellows) identified in prior runs but not captured in Feb 24 Exa search.
